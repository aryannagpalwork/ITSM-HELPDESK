## Account & Access
### Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Follow the [Password Reset SOP](../IT/Password_Reset_SOP.md). For self‑service, go to [https://okta.enterprisetechsolutions.com](https://okta.enterprisetechsolutions.com) and click "Forgot password?"

### Q: How do I set up MFA?
A: Follow the [MFA Setup Guide](../IT/MFA_Setup_Guide.md). All users are required to set up MFA within 7 days of account creation.

### Q: I lost my MFA device. What do I do?
A: Contact IT Support immediately at +1 (800) 555-1234. IT Support will unenroll your old device, and you can set up MFA on a new device. See [MFA Setup Guide – Reset MFA Device](../IT/MFA_Setup_Guide.md#reset-mfa-device).

### Q: My account is locked. What do I do?
A: Wait 30 minutes, or contact IT Support for manual unlock. See [Password Lockout Runbook](../Incident_Runbooks/Password_Lockout_Runbook.md).

### Q: How do I request access to a system (e.g., Jira, GitHub, AWS)?
A: Submit a ServiceNow request at [https://itsm.enterprisetechsolutions.com](https://itsm.enterprisetechsolutions.com). Include the system name, business justification, and your manager's approval.

## Equipment
### Q: How do I get a new laptop or replacement equipment?
A: Submit a ServiceNow request. See [Laptop Provisioning Guide](../IT/Laptop_Provisioning_Guide.md) or [Laptop Replacement Process](../Hardware/Laptop_Replacement_Process.md).

### Q: My laptop is broken. What do I do?
A: Contact IT Support immediately. Do not attempt to repair it yourself.

### Q: Can I use a personal laptop for work?
A: Generally, no. Company work must be done on company‑issued equipment. Exceptions may be granted by IT Security and your manager on a case‑by‑case basis.

## Email & Communication
### Q: How do I access my email?
A: Webmail: [https://outlook.office.com](https://outlook.office.com). You can also set up Outlook on your laptop or mobile device.

### Q: What are the email size limits?
A: 50MB per email. For larger files, use SharePoint or OneDrive for Business.

### Q: How do I set up my email on my phone?
A: Use the Microsoft Outlook app from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in with your EnterpriseTech email and Okta credentials.

## Network & Connectivity
### Q: How do I connect to the office Wi-Fi?
A: Follow the [Wi‑Fi Troubleshooting Guide – Connect to ETSI-Employee](../Networking/WiFi_Troubleshooting.md#step-3-connect-to-etsi-employee).

### Q: How do I set up VPN?
A: Follow the [VPN Reset Guide](../IT/VPN_Reset_Guide.md). VPN is required for accessing internal systems remotely.

### Q: Does EnterpriseTech support Wi-Fi 6E?
A: Yes! Wi-Fi 6E (6GHz) is available in most offices. See [Wi‑Fi Troubleshooting Guide](../Networking/WiFi_Troubleshooting.md) for details.

## Software
### Q: How do I install software on my laptop?
A: Use Company Portal (Windows) or Self Service (macOS) for approved software. For unapproved software, submit a ServiceNow request. See [Software Installation Guide](../IT/Software_Installation_Guide.md).

### Q: Can I use ChatGPT or other AI tools for work?
A: Yes, but only with approved tools and never with sensitive data. See [Acceptable Use Policy – AI Usage Guidelines](../Policies/Acceptable_Use_Policy.md#ai-usage-guidelines).

## Security
### Q: What should I do if I receive a suspicious email or phishing attempt?
A: Do not click any links or download any attachments. Forward the email to [phish@enterprisetechsolutions.com](mailto:phish@enterprisetechsolutions.com) and then delete it. See [Incident Response Playbook](../Security/Incident_Response_Playbook.md).

### Q: How do I classify data?
A: Follow the [Data Classification Policy](../Security/Data_Classification_Policy.md). Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.

### Q: What is the company password policy?
A: See [Password Policy](../Security/Password_Policy.md). Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, or 20 characters for passphrases.

## Training
### Q: Are there mandatory IT security training courses?
A: Yes! All employees must complete annual security awareness training. New employees must complete training during onboarding. Training is available via the Learning Management System (LMS) at [https://lms.enterprisetechsolutions.com](https://lms.enterprisetechsolutions.com).

## General
### Q: What is the IT Support Desk phone number and email?

### Q: What are IT Support hours?
