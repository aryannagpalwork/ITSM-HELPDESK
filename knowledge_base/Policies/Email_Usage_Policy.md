## Purpose
This policy defines acceptable and unacceptable use of EnterpriseTech Solutions email systems.

## Scope
All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with EnterpriseTech email accounts.

## Acceptable Use
- Use email for job-related communication
- Use email for occasional personal communication (does not interfere with work)
- Use appropriate and professional language
- Include a signature with your name, title, and contact information

## Unacceptable Use
- Use email for illegal activities
- Send spam or chain letters
- Send harassing, discriminatory, or offensive messages
- Use email for personal business ventures without prior approval
- Share sensitive company data via email (use SharePoint/OneDrive instead)
- Open attachments or click links from unknown senders
- Disable email security features
- Use AI tools to generate emails containing sensitive data without review
- Impersonate someone else via email

## Phishing Response
If you receive a suspicious email:
1. Do NOT click any links or download any attachments
2. Do NOT reply to the email
3. Forward the email to phish@enterprisetechsolutions.com
4. Delete the email
5. IT Security will investigate and notify you

## Email Retention
- Work-related emails: Retained for 7 years
- Emails with legal holds: Retained indefinitely
- Personal emails: Should be kept minimal; employees are responsible for backing up personal emails if needed

## Best Practices
- Be careful with attachments and links
- Use strong passwords and MFA
- Do not share your email password with anyone
- Use SharePoint/OneDrive for large files and sensitive data
- Review emails before sending to ensure no sensitive data is included
- If using AI to generate emails, review carefully for accuracy and data leakage

## Warnings
- **WARNING**: Phishing emails are a common attack vector – always verify senders and links.
- **WARNING**: Sending sensitive data via email violates the Data Classification Policy.
