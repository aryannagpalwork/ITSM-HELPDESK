## Purpose
This policy defines acceptable and unacceptable use of EnterpriseTech Solutions information systems, networks, and equipment to protect company assets and ensure a safe, productive work environment.

## Scope
This policy applies to all users of EnterpriseTech Solutions information systems, including:
- Full-time and part-time employees
- Contractors and temporary workers
- Third-party vendors and partners
- Board members and advisors
- Visitors with guest network access

## Acceptable Use
Users may use EnterpriseTech systems, networks, and equipment for:
1. Job-related tasks and responsibilities
2. Occasional personal use that does not interfere with work productivity or violate any other policy
3. Approved professional development activities
4. Communication with colleagues, customers, and partners (in accordance with [Email Usage Policy](./Email_Usage_Policy.md))

## Unacceptable Use
Users must NOT:
1. Access systems or data they are not authorized to access
2. Share passwords or MFA devices with anyone (see [Password Policy](../Security/Password_Policy.md) and [MFA Policy](../Security/MFA_Policy.md))
3. Use company systems for illegal activities
4. Download or install unauthorized software (see [Software Installation Guide](../IT/Software_Installation_Guide.md))
5. Visit malicious or inappropriate websites (pornography, gambling, hate speech, etc.)
6. Send spam, phishing, or harassing messages
7. Use company systems for personal business ventures or commercial activities without prior approval
8. Exfiltrate company data via unauthorized channels (personal email, cloud storage, USB drives, etc.)
9. Use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot) with sensitive company data unless approved by IT Security
10. Violate intellectual property laws or company copyright policies
11. Disable or bypass security controls (antivirus, firewall, MFA, etc.)
12. Use company equipment for mining cryptocurrency
13. Record audio or video in the workplace without prior consent (where legally required)
14. Engage in cyberbullying, harassment, or discrimination via company systems

## AI Usage Guidelines
AI tools may be used for productivity purposes, provided:
- No sensitive or proprietary data is entered into the AI tool (see [Data Classification Policy](../Security/Data_Classification_Policy.md) for data classification)
- The AI tool is approved by IT Security (approved tools list available on IT Portal)
- AI-generated content is reviewed and verified for accuracy before use
- AI tools are not used to write code that will be deployed to production without thorough review and testing

## Consequences of Violation
Violations of this policy may result in:
1. Verbal or written warning
2. Suspension of system access
3. Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract
4. Legal action if the violation involves illegal activities
5. Reporting to law enforcement if applicable

## Reporting Violations
Users must report suspected policy violations to:
- IT Security Team: security@enterprisetechsolutions.com
- HR Business Partner
- Ethics Hotline (anonymous): 1-888-555-ETHICS | [https://ethics.enterprisetechsolutions.com](https://ethics.enterprisetechsolutions.com)

## Monitoring
EnterpriseTech Solutions reserves the right to monitor all activity on company systems and networks, including:
- Email
- Internet usage
- File access and transfer
- Software installation
- Device location (for company-owned mobile devices)

## Best Practices
- Use company systems primarily for work purposes
- Be mindful of data security and privacy
- Keep software and operating systems updated
- Report suspicious activity immediately
- Review and understand all company policies

## Warnings
- **WARNING**: Company systems are for business use. Personal use should be limited and non-disruptive.
- **WARNING**: All activity on company systems may be monitored.
- **WARNING**: Violations of this policy may result in termination of employment or contract.

## Policy Review
This policy is reviewed annually by the IT Security Team and HR Department.
