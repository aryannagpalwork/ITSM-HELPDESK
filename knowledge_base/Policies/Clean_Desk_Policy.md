## Purpose
This policy ensures sensitive company information is protected by requiring employees to keep their desks clean and secure at all times.

## Scope
All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors working in EnterpriseTech offices or remotely.

## Policy Requirements
### Office Work
- At the end of each workday and when leaving your desk for extended periods (30+ minutes):
  - Lock your computer/laptop
  - Store all sensitive documents in locked drawers or cabinets
  - Put away USB drives, external hard drives, and other storage devices
  - Shred any unneeded sensitive documents
  - Log out of all applications

### Remote Work
- Keep your work area secure at home
- Do not leave sensitive documents visible to others
- Lock your computer when not in use
- Store physical sensitive documents in a secure location

## Shredding Guidelines
- Use company shredders for sensitive documents
- Sensitive documents include:
  - Papers with customer PII
  - Financial documents
  - Internal reports with confidential information
  - Any document marked "Restricted" or "Confidential"
- If no shredder is available, contact IT or Admin for assistance

## Best Practices
- Use screen protectors to prevent shoulder surfing
- Do not write passwords on sticky notes
- Lock your computer even if you're leaving for just a minute
- Shred sensitive documents immediately after use
- Keep your work area organized

## Warnings
- **WARNING**: Failure to follow this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- **WARNING**: If you see an unattended desk with sensitive documents, secure them and notify IT Security immediately.
