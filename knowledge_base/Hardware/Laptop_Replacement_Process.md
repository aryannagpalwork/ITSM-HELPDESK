## Purpose
This guide outlines the standard process for replacing company-issued laptops at EnterpriseTech Solutions.

## Scope
All full-time and part-time employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with company-issued laptops.

## When to Replace a Laptop
- Laptop is 3+ years old
- Laptop is broken and cannot be repaired cost-effectively
- Laptop no longer meets performance requirements for the role
- Laptop is lost or stolen (see [Device Loss/Theft Policy](../Security/Device_Loss_Theft_Policy.md))

## Replacement Options
| Option | Eligibility | Specs |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Standard Laptop | All employees | 14" display, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, Intel i5 or M3 |
| Performance Laptop | Engineers, designers, power users | 16" display, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD, Intel i7 or M3 Pro |
| Developer Laptop | Software engineers | 16" display, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD, Intel i9 or M3 Max |

## Laptop Replacement Process
### Step 1: Submit a Request
1. Go to [ServiceNow](https://itsm.enterprisetechsolutions.com)
2. Create a new "Laptop Replacement" request
3. Include:
   - Current laptop model/serial number
   - Reason for replacement
   - Desired laptop option (Standard, Performance, Developer)
   - Manager's approval

### Step 2: IT Review & Approval
1. IT Support will review the request within 2 business days
2. If approved, IT will order the new laptop
3. If not approved, IT will contact you to discuss alternatives

### Step 3: Prepare for Replacement
1. Back up all your data:
   - Save important files to OneDrive for Business or SharePoint
   - Export bookmarks, settings, etc.
   - Do NOT store sensitive data locally (see [Data Classification Policy](../Security/Data_Classification_Policy.md))
2. IT will provide a data backup service if needed (request via ServiceNow)

### Step 4: Receive New Laptop
1. IT will contact you when your new laptop is ready
2. Pick up from IT office (in-person) or receive via FedEx (remote)
3. Sign laptop receipt form

### Step 5: Set Up New Laptop
1. Follow [Laptop Provisioning Guide](../IT/Laptop_Provisioning_Guide.md)
2. Log in with Okta credentials
3. Restore your data from OneDrive/SharePoint
4. Install approved software via Company Portal/Self Service
5. Contact IT Support if you need help

### Step 6: Return Old Laptop
1. Back up any remaining data (double-check!)
2. Wipe the old laptop using IT-approved wipe tool (available via Company Portal)
3. Return old laptop to IT office or ship via FedEx (pre-paid label provided)
4. IT will verify the wipe and process the return

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "How do I back up my data?" | Use OneDrive for Business/SharePoint, or request IT backup service via ServiceNow |
| "My new laptop isn't working" | Contact IT Support immediately |
| "I lost my old laptop" | See [Device Loss/Theft Policy](../Security/Device_Loss_Theft_Policy.md) – report to IT and Security immediately |

## Best Practices
- Back up your data regularly (not just when replacing laptop)
- Return old laptop within 5 business days of receiving new one
- Do not attempt to wipe the laptop yourself – use IT-approved tool
- Keep your laptop in good condition to extend its lifespan

## Warnings
- **WARNING**: Do NOT store sensitive data on your laptop local disk – use OneDrive/SharePoint.
- **WARNING**: You must return your old laptop – failure to do so may result in deductions from your final paycheck (if applicable).
- **WARNING**: If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to IT and Security within 1 hour.

## Escalation Path
1. IT Support Desk
2. IT Support Manager (Sarah Jenkins)
3. IT Director

## Notes
- Laptops are replaced every 3 years as part of the standard refresh cycle
- Remote employees will receive new laptops via FedEx with pre-paid return label for old laptop
- All returned laptops are securely wiped and recycled or donated (if in good condition)
