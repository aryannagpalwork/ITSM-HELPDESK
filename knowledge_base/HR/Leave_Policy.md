## Purpose
This policy outlines the different types of leave available to EnterpriseTech Solutions employees and how to request leave.

## Scope
All full-time and part-time employees. Contractors should refer to their contract for leave terms.

## Types of Leave
### Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Eligibility**: All full-time employees
- **Accrual**: 15 days per year (pro-rated for new hires)
- **Carryover**: Up to 5 days can be carried over to next year
- **Requesting**: Submit request via HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance (or as soon as possible for emergencies)

### Sick Leave
- **Eligibility**: All employees
- **Accrual**: 10 days per year
- **Use**: For personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for sick family members
- **Requesting**: Notify your manager as soon as possible; submit formal request via HR portal

### Parental Leave
- **Eligibility**: All full-time employees with at least 1 year of service
- **Duration**: 12 weeks paid parental leave for birth, adoption, or foster care
- **Requesting**: Submit request via HR portal at least 3 months in advance

### Bereavement Leave
- **Eligibility**: All employees
- **Duration**: Up to 5 days paid leave for immediate family members; up to 2 days for extended family
- **Requesting**: Notify your manager as soon as possible; submit formal request via HR portal

### Paid Holidays
EnterpriseTech observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day After Thanksgiving
- Christmas Day

## Requesting Leave
1. Go to [HR Portal](https://hr.enterprisetechsolutions.com)
2. Click "Request Leave"
3. Select leave type
4. Enter dates
5. Add comments (if needed)
6. Submit for manager approval
7. Manager will approve/reject within 3 business days

## Best Practices
- Request leave as far in advance as possible
- Plan handover of work before going on leave
- Set up out-of-office reply in email and Slack/Teams
- Notify key stakeholders of your absence

## Warnings
- **WARNING**: Abuse of leave policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- **WARNING**: Failure to give proper notice for leave may result in denied request or disciplinary action.
