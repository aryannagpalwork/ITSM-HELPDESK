## Purpose
This guide outlines the standard onboarding process for new employees at EnterpriseTech Solutions to ensure a smooth, consistent, and welcoming start.

## Scope
All full-time and part-time employees, excluding contractors (see separate [Vendor Onboarding Guide](./Vendor_Onboarding_Guide.md)).

## Prerequisites
- Signed offer letter
- Completed background check
- Completed I-9 and tax forms
- Employee ID assigned
- IT equipment requested (via HR portal at least 7 days before start date)

## Onboarding Timeline
| Day | Tasks |
|-----|-------|
| -7 | IT prepares equipment, account creation begins |
| -3 | HR sends welcome email with first-day instructions |
| 1 | Orientation, IT setup, meet team |
| 2 | Benefits enrollment, policy training |
| 5 | Probationary period review meeting scheduled |
| 30 | First 30-day check-in with manager |
| 60 | Second 60-day check-in |
| 90 | Probationary period end, performance review |

## Day 1 Agenda
### Morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
1. **Welcome & Orientation (9:00–10:00 AM)**:
   - HR welcome
   - Company mission, vision, values
   - Organizational structure overview
   - DEI orientation session
2. **IT Setup (10:00–11:30 AM)**:
   - Receive laptop, monitor, docking station (see [Laptop Provisioning Guide](../IT/Laptop_Provisioning_Guide.md))
   - Okta account setup, MFA enrollment (see [MFA Setup Guide](../IT/MFA_Setup_Guide.md))
   - Email, Slack, Microsoft Teams setup
   - 1Password setup (see [Password Policy](../Security/Password_Policy.md))
   - IT tour of key systems (ServiceNow, Confluence, Jira)
3. **Meet & Greet (11:30 AM–12:00 PM)**:
   - Introductions to team members
   - Tour of office (if in-person)

### Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM)
1. **Manager 1:1 (1:00–2:00 PM)**:
   - Review of role and responsibilities
   - 30-60-90 day goals
   - Team expectations
2. **Policy Training (2:00–4:00 PM)**:
   - [Acceptable Use Policy](../Policies/Acceptable_Use_Policy.md)
   - [Clean Desk Policy](../Policies/Clean_Desk_Policy.md)
   - [Email Usage Policy](../Policies/Email_Usage_Policy.md)
   - [Data Classification Policy](../Security/Data_Classification_Policy.md)
3. **Benefits Overview (4:00–5:00 PM)**:
   - Health, dental, vision insurance
   - 401(k) plan
   - PTO and leave policies (see [Leave Policy](./Leave_Policy.md))

## First Week Tasks
- Complete benefits enrollment within 5 days
- Complete policy training
- Schedule 1:1s with key team members
- Review team Confluence space
- Set up desk (if in-person)
- Order any additional equipment via ServiceNow

## Manager Responsibilities
- Create 30-60-90 day goals
- Schedule regular 1:1s (weekly)
- Introduce new hire to key stakeholders
- Ensure new hire has all necessary tools and access
- Conduct 30, 60, and 90-day check-ins

## New Hire Responsibilities
- Complete all onboarding tasks
- Ask questions!
- Review policies and procedures
- Set up 1:1s with team members
- Provide feedback on onboarding experience

## Troubleshooting
| Issue | Contact |

| Payroll questions | Payroll Team |
| Policy questions | HR Business Partner |
| Team/role questions | Direct Manager |

## Best Practices
- Start preparing for onboarding at least 2 weeks before start date
- Assign a "buddy" to help new hire acclimate
- Schedule regular check-ins during first 90 days
- Provide clear expectations and goals
- Encourage questions and feedback

## Warnings
- **WARNING**: IT equipment must be requested at least 7 days before start date to ensure availability.
- **WARNING**: Benefits enrollment must be completed within 5 days of start date; otherwise, default coverage will apply.

## Escalation Path
1. Direct Manager
2. HR Business Partner
3. HR Director
